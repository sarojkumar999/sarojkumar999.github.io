About

I'm an AI/ML researcher, architect and innovator at Wipro Limited, with more than twelve years of experience designing and delivering artificial-intelligence systems.

As a forward deployed engineer, I work directly with clients to take Generative AI and Agentic AI from idea to production. My work sits where research meets the field: building computer-vision and analytics solutions for safety, security and operations in heavy industry; applying AI to precision agriculture and 3D point-cloud volumetric analysis; and exploring what generative, agentic and quantum-inspired methods can do for real organisations.

Alongside consulting and architecture work, I speak regularly at faculty development programmes and industry events, and I enjoy mentoring the next generation of AI practitioners.
